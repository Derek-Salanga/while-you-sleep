## What the app is

Each day, you and your partner record a short video answering a shared
question. Once you've both posted for the day, you can watch each other's
clip. It's built around exactly two people per pair, joined by an invite code
you share with each other yourselves, so there's no public feed and no
strangers.

## Reporting and blocking

If your partner sends you something that violates these terms, you can:

- **Report a clip**: open it and tap "Report" near the date
- **Block your partner**: go to Settings, then Account, then "Block [name]"

Reporting flags the clip for us to review. Blocking immediately ends your
pairing: your partner will not be able to pair with you again, and your
shared history (clips, reactions, trip, anniversary) is deleted for both of
you.

We review every report within 24 hours. If we find a violation of these
terms, we may remove the reported content and ban the account responsible,
including signing them out and preventing them from using the app again.

## Account deletion

You can delete your account at any time from Settings, then Account, then
Delete account. This permanently removes your account and the shared history
you and your partner built together, including their copy of it. This cannot
be undone. See our [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md) for details on what we
collect and how deletion works.
